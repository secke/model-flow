# Experiment 07 — Z2 p-values by simulation

**Question.** Experiments 05 and 06 read the Acerbi-Székely Z2 statistic against fixed thresholds, −0.70 (5%) and −1.8 (0.01%), and quantparity's validation found that these reject correct Student-t 3 models too often. What changes when each Z2 is given a p-value simulated from the model's own forecast distribution, as Acerbi and Székely recommend? Do the conclusions of 05 and 06 hold?

**Date run:** 2026-11-06, rerun under v0.20 on 2026-11-07 · **ModelFlow:** v0.19.0, v0.20.0 · **Engine:** quantparity `ab2c201` (0.4.0)

## What this experiment added

| quantparity 0.4.0 | Reference check |
|---|---|
| `Z2Null`: the null distribution of Z2 for a set of VaR / ES forecasts. Each day's standardized law is drawn: normal, unit-variance Student-t, or the past standardized returns of that day for filtered historical simulation. σ_t cancels out of Z2. The forecasts must match the model they record. Results are reproducible from the seed | P(Z2 ≤ −0.70) and P(Z2 ≤ −1.8) on 250 days for normal, t3, t5 against an independent numpy / scipy Monte Carlo: gap 1.2e-3, Monte Carlo standard error 8e-4. On 1,000 samples generated by the forecast model itself, the simulated test rejects 5.8%, 4.9% and 5.0% at 5% (normal, t5, t3), against 7.2% for the fixed threshold at t3 ([`validation/RESULTS.md`](https://github.com/secke/quantparity/blob/main/validation/RESULTS.md)) |

In ModelFlow, `es_backtest` takes `simulations` and `seed`. It then adds a `simulated` block (p-value, simulated 5% and 0.01% quantiles, zone), and gives a p-value and a simulated zone to each 250-day window. The step status follows the simulated zones: green at p ≥ 5%, yellow at p ≥ 0.01%, red below. Simulation needs forecasts from a `var_model` step, because an external model's forecast distribution is not known. Since v0.20 it is the default for such forecasts (finding 6), and the whole-sample zone is the simulated one; without simulation, a sample longer than 250 days takes the zone of its latest window instead of the 250-day thresholds (`zone_basis`).

## Set-up

[`ewma_models.toml`](ewma_models.toml) and [`garch_models.toml`](garch_models.toml) are the pipelines of experiments 05 and 06, on the same days: 2,231 days from 2017-12-12, and 1,761 days from 2019-11-11. The only change is `simulations = 100000, seed = 1` in every `es_backtest` step. The forecasts and the VaR backtests are identical to those of 05 and 06 (`mf diff`: "same output"), and so are the Z2 values. Each model gets 9 tests (8 for experiment 06's period): the whole sample and each 250-day window, 118 in all, each against 100,000 samples of its own null distribution.

**Findings.**

1. **The fixed thresholds are for 250 days, and are wrong on a whole sample.** −0.70 and −1.8 are the 5% and 0.01% quantiles of Z2 on one year. Z2 is an average over the days, so its null distribution tightens with the length of the sample. Over 1,761 to 2,231 days the simulated 5% quantile is −0.22 to −0.27 and the 0.01% quantile −0.53 to −0.98. ModelFlow's `es_backtest` applies the fixed thresholds to the whole sample as well as to the windows, and so did experiments 05 and 06. Seven of the 14 whole-sample verdicts change: the Student-t 5 and 4 challengers of 05 and GARCH with a normal quantile go from green to red (p ≤ 3e-5), the champion (on both periods) and GJR normal from yellow to red, GJR t5 from green to yellow.
2. **This reverses a finding of experiment 05.** There, the Student-t 5 and 4 challengers failed Kupiec while their Z2 was green over the whole period, and the explanation was that frequency and severity offset each other. They do not: the simulated p-value is below 1e-4 for both, and the ES backtest rejects them as clearly as the VaR backtest. The offset is real, Z2 is −0.66 instead of −0.98 for the champion, but not enough. [Experiment 05's README](../05-fat-tails-es-backtest/README.md) now says so.
3. **On 250-day windows, the fixed thresholds hold.** Of the 104 window tests, one changes zone: GJR normal from November 2020 to November 2021, Z2 = −0.704 against a threshold of −0.70, p = 0.051. The simulated 5% quantile of a 250-day window is −0.70 for the normal (quantparity validation), so Acerbi and Székely's thresholds are right where they were designed to be used. For the Student-t 3 model the simulated 5% threshold of a window is lower, about −0.81, but none of its eight windows falls between the two: the closest, Z2 = −0.89, has p = 0.040.
4. **The conclusions on filtered historical simulation stand.** All five FHS models have p = 0.27 to 0.96 on the whole sample, and green windows wherever they were green before. The only challengers that pass the ES backtest are still the filtered simulations.
5. **A red zone needs about 100,000 simulations.** With 10,000, the smallest p-value is 1e-4, the red threshold itself. A first run at 10,000 gave GARCH normal's Covid window (Z2 = −1.72) a red zone because no simulated value was lower. With 200,000 the p-value is 1.85e-4 (yellow), and with 100,000 the zone is yellow too. The heavier the tail, the more simulations: the 0.01% quantile of the t3 model on 250 days moves by several tenths between seeds even at 200,000 samples.

6. **The default of v0.20 gives the same 118 zones in half the time.** Without a `simulations` parameter, ModelFlow simulates 10,000 samples and goes to 100,000 only when the p-value is near a threshold: below 0.1%, or between 4% and 6%. On the two pipelines, 34 s instead of 67 s on 8 threads, and every zone, whole sample and windows, equals the one of 100,000 simulations. A first version extended only below 0.1%: it put the GJR window at p = 0.051 in yellow, which is why the band around 5% was added.

## What this experiment changed in ModelFlow

| Finding | Status |
|---|---|
| Fixed Z2 thresholds are too severe for very fat tails (experiment 05) | **added** in quantparity 0.4.0 / ModelFlow v0.19.0: `es_backtest` with `simulations`: simulated p-values and zones, whole sample and windows |
| The fixed thresholds were applied to whole samples of 1,761 and 2,231 days, where they are far too lenient | **fixed** in v0.20.0: simulated by default for `var_model` forecasts; otherwise a longer sample takes the zone of its latest 250-day window (`zone_basis`) |
| A red zone with 10,000 simulations is a resolution artefact | **fixed** in v0.20.0: the default simulates 10,000 samples and 100,000 when p is near a threshold (finding 6) |
